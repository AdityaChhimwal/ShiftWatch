Retain attribution/license information and identify modifications when sharing derived material. This notice concerns the source dataset.

Fanaee-T, Hadi, and Gama, Joao. *Event labeling combining ensemble detectors and background knowledge.* Progress in Artificial Intelligence (2013). <https://doi.org/10.1007/s13748-013-0040-3>.

The initial source snapshot retains the original ZIP and extracted bytes under ignored `data/raw/bike-sharing/`. [The source record](reports/source.json) retains the retrieval time, URLs, fingerprints, and headers. ShiftWatch selects hourly observations, projects a subset of fields, and assigns separate chronological roles. The bundled demo pool contains projected development observations and their outcomes. Controlled replays sample from those observations and can alter humidity or outcomes to simulate incidents. The original archive contains aggregated historical Capital Bikeshare data.
